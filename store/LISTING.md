# Store listing — Parent Playbook

Copy for App Store Connect and the Google Play Console. Character limits are noted, and every line
below fits inside them. Voice: dad-to-dad, learning together. No claims the app can't back up: no
"improves performance", no medical or injury-prevention promises, no "AI coach".

## Apple App Store

**Subtitle** (30): `Weekly plans for kid athletes`

**Promotional text** (170, can change without a review):
> Football season is here. Get a week of short, age-right practice for your kid, built around their team schedule, in about three minutes.

**Keywords** (100, comma-separated, no spaces):

youth sports,football,basketball,soccer,baseball,practice,drills,coach,kids,training,season,team,dad

**Description** (4,000):

> My son is six and in his first year of tackle football. After practice he'd ask me what he should work on at home, and I didn't have a good answer. Most of the training content out there is built for teenagers, and I wasn't going to hand a six-year-old a high-school workout.
>
> So I built the thing I wanted: a plan for the week, sized for my kid's age and sport.
>
> **HOW IT WORKS**
> Tell it your athlete's age, sport, position and practice days. In about three minutes you get a week: one short thing to do each day, planned around team practice and game day, with rest days that count as done. Most sessions take 5 to 15 minutes in a living room or driveway with no equipment.
>
> **ONE THING TODAY**
> Open the app and today's session is at the top: what to do, how long it takes, and how to do it. Tap Mark done. The rest of the week sits underneath.
>
> **BUILT FOR THEIR AGE**
> Age decides what's safe. A 7-year-old and a 14-year-old don't get the same plan. If you log height, the app watches for a growth spurt and eases off when one shows up.
>
> **THEIR JOURNEY, KEPT PRIVATE**
> Log games, stats, milestones like a first touchdown, and photos. It all stays in a private journey that only you and the family you invite can see.
>
> **BRING THE FAMILY, AND THE COACH IF YOU WANT**
> Invite a co-parent or grandparent. Connect your kid's coach with a team code if you want them to see age, sport and measurements. You decide what anyone else sees, and you can take it back.
>
> **GROWTH, WITHOUT THE PRESSURE**
> Track the measurements you choose. An optional photo scan works out wingspan, reach and a few other lengths on your phone. The photos are never uploaded, and nothing is measured without your permission first.
>
> **REMINDERS THAT RESPECT YOUR EVENINGS**
> A Sunday note when the new week is ready, a game-day reminder, quiet hours overnight, and never more than four a week.
>
> **YOUR KID'S DATA STAYS YOURS**
> Your child never has an account and is never public. No ads, no selling data, no tracking. Delete a profile or your whole account from inside the app, any time.
>
> We're a family company, Brown Pig LLC, and we're figuring this out as parents too. Tell us what's working: support@playbookbp.com

**What's New** (first version):
> First release on the App Store. Same Parent Playbook as playbookbp.com, now with reminders on your lock screen, the share sheet for reports and reels, and invite links that open straight into the app.
