What this proves

The page proves that Parent Playbook can accept a documented provider, reject undeclared or malformed values, display scalar estimates with their uncertainty disclosure, calculate descriptive bias, MAE and RMSE against independent DEXA references, and hold accepted results in an organization-scoped store with tenant isolation, audited mutations and bounded retention. It does not prove the provider’s evidence, clinical validity, DEXA equivalence or accuracy for a population, and no composition result exists to store today.
