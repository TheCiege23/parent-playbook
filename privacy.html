Privacy Policy

Parent Playbook stores information about children. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it — in plain language, because you should be able to read it in a few minutes.

Last updated 13 September 2026

The short version. Your child never logs in and is never public. Only you and the adults you personally invite can see your child's profile. We do not sell your data, we do not show advertising, we do not use your family's information to train AI models, and we do not share it with anyone except the infrastructure providers listed below that are needed to run the app. You can delete a child's profile — and everything attached to it — at any time, from inside the app.

Who we are

Parent Playbook ("we", "us") is a training-plan app for parents of young athletes, operated at playbookbp.com. The service is directed at parents and legal guardians, not at children.

Questions, requests, or complaints: support@playbookbp.com.

Information about you

  • Email address — to create your account, confirm it, and reset your password.
  • Password — handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a secure hash. We never see it.
  • Your display name, and optionally whether you played the sport yourself (used to tailor coaching notes to you).
  • Your own training record (optional, where available) — if you use Adult Training: your goals, a gym or activity schedule, workouts you tick off, and weight or tape measurements you type in yourself. It is private to you: co-parents, family members, coaches and organizations cannot see it. Nothing in it is estimated from a photo, and it is deleted with your account.
  • Push notification subscription — only if you turn notifications on.

What we do not collect

  • No location tracking.
  • No contacts and no microphone. The camera is used only for a photo you deliberately take — one you choose to upload, or the four scan frames, which are measured on your phone and never sent to us.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics, no tracking pixels, and no session recording of any kind.
  • Nothing at all directly from your child — every field is entered by you.

How we use it

We use your family's information for exactly one purpose: to build and show your child's training plan, and to track the progress you choose to record. If you keep your own training record, it is used only to show it back to you. That includes sending you account emails (confirmation, password reset, co-parent invitations) and notifications you have opted into.

We do not use it for advertising, profiling, resale, or to train machine-learning models.

Aggregate feature usage

We keep a running daily count of which parts of the app get used — for example, "the body scan was opened 40 times today." Each count is a single number attached to a feature name and a date. Nothing else is attached to it: no account, no child, no device, no IP address, and no way to work backward from a count to a person. This is not the third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or session recording described above — it never leaves our own database, no outside company ever sees it, and it cannot show what any individual did. It exists only to tell us which parts of the app are actually useful, so we can build more of what helps and less of what doesn't.

Who can see your child's profile

  • You.
  • Adults you explicitly invite — a co-parent, a grandparent — using an invite link you generate. You can revoke their access at any time.
  • A coach or trainer you invite through Team Connect sees a deliberately narrower slice — age, sport, and measurements only. Never games, journey entries, or photos, and never anyone you haven't personally invited. This access is enforced the same way as everything else on this page: at the database level, not just hidden in the app, and revocable by you at any time.
  • A team or organization you join with a code — a coach shares a code for their team, league, or club, and entering it adds your child to that roster. The organization's admin sees the same narrow slice a coach does (age, sport, measurements) for your child alongside the rest of the roster, plus which coaches are connected to which athletes on it — never a coach's messages, and never games, journey entries, or photos. Nobody is added to a roster without the code; there is no way for an organization to search for or browse athletes.
  • Nobody else. Profiles are not public, not searchable, and not shared between families.

Access is enforced in our database itself, not merely hidden in the app: every table carries row-level security rules, so a request for another family's data is refused by the database regardless of what the app asks for.

Photos and videos

Media you upload is stored in a private bucket, not on the public internet. It is retrievable only through short-lived signed links generated for people who already have access to that child. Deleting the child's profile deletes the underlying files.

Body scanning

The app can work out a few body measurements from four photographs you take. This section is the written policy for that feature, including how long the results are kept and when they are destroyed. It applies whether the person measured is an adult or a child.

The photographs are never uploaded, and never stored

The measuring runs on your own phone, inside your browser. The four frames are held in memory only for the seconds the measurement takes and are then discarded. They are not sent to us, not sent to any third party, and not written to storage anywhere. No record we hold points at a photograph, because no photograph is ever created on our side. A measuring model is downloaded to your phone the first time you scan; your pictures never travel the other way.

What is measured, and what is not

Five lengths: wingspan, standing reach, shoulder width, torso length and leg length. These are distances between points on the body — the same things a tape measure produces — scaled against a height you measured and entered yourself.

We do not measure, estimate, derive or store: chest, waist or hip circumference; body fat, body composition, or BMI; any facial template, fingerprint, iris or voice print; or any other identifier capable of recognising a person. The feature cannot do these things — it is not a limit we apply to a system that could.

Your written permission comes first

No scan can start without it. Before the camera opens we show you exactly what will be measured, where it is processed, and the date the results will be deleted, and we record your agreement against that specific wording. Where the person being measured is a minor, that permission must come from a parent or legal guardian; where they are an adult, it is theirs to give. If we later change what the feature does or how it works, the previous permission stops applying and we ask again rather than carrying it forward.

Retention schedule and destruction

Scan measurements are retained for twelve months from the date of the scan, and are destroyed at the end of that period. Scanning the same athlete again starts a fresh twelve months for the new measurements. They are also destroyed sooner in any of these cases, whichever comes first:

  • You delete the measurement from the Growth tab: tap the measurement, then Delete on the reading. Where a set of readings was saved together as one body scan, Growth sends you to the Scan tab, where the whole scan is deleted at once.
  • You delete the athlete's profile, or your account.
  • The purpose for collecting them is satisfied — that is, the athlete's profile is no longer being used to build a training plan.

You can withdraw permission at any time from Account → Body scan permissions. Withdrawal takes effect immediately and stops any further scanning of that athlete. It does not by itself erase measurements you have already saved, because those are part of the growth record you have been deliberately keeping — you delete those from the Growth tab, the same way as any other measurement (a set saved as one body scan is deleted whole, from the Scan tab), or by deleting the profile.

We never sell it, and never profit from it

We do not sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from a person's body measurements, and we do not disclose them to anyone except the adults you have given access to that athlete. They are not used for advertising, not used to train machine-learning models, and not combined with data from any other source.

Body measurements derived from photographs may constitute biometric information under the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (740 ILCS 14), the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act (Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 503.001), and Washington's My Health My Data Act. This section is published to satisfy the written-policy, retention-schedule and destruction requirements those laws impose.

A note on error reporting

When something goes wrong in the app, a report is sent to Sentry so we can fix it. We have deliberately configured this more narrowly than most apps do, because this one holds information about children:

  • No session recording. We do not use screen replay of any kind. Nothing that appears on your screen is ever recorded.
  • No identifying information. Reports are not tagged with your account, your email, or your child's name.
  • No console output. Application logs are excluded entirely, because they can contain the data being processed at the time.
  • Addresses are trimmed. Web addresses in a report are reduced to the page path — any identifiers in the query string are removed before sending.
  • No request or response bodies. The contents of what the app sends to and receives from our database — which is where your child's details actually travel — are excluded entirely.
  • No variable capture. Error reports normally include the values held in memory where the fault happened. That is switched off, because those values can be your child's record.
  • No IP address stored. Error reporting services normally record the IP address a report was sent from. We have turned that off, so the address is discarded rather than retained.
  • A final scrub. Anything still resembling an email address or an access token is redacted before the report leaves your device.

What a report does contain: the error message, the technical stack trace, the page it happened on, and your browser and operating system version. That is enough to fix the fault and no more.

We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone, and we have no advertising partners.

Children's privacy and COPPA

Because this app is about children under 13, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act applies. Our commitments:

  • We collect information about a child only after a parent or legal guardian gives consent during setup. That consent is recorded together with the child's profile in a single database transaction, so a profile cannot exist without its consent record.
  • We collect only what the training plan actually needs. Height, weight, photos and extra measurements are all optional, and the app works fully without them.
  • We never condition your child's participation on providing more information than is reasonably necessary.
  • Children cannot create accounts, log in, message anyone, or be contacted through the app.
  • As a parent you may review the information we hold about your child, delete it, or refuse further collection at any time.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single measurement — in the app, on the athlete's Growth tab: tap the measurement, then Delete on the reading you want gone. Where a set of readings was saved together as one body scan, Growth sends you to the Scan tab, where the whole scan is deleted at once. Either way the record is removed, not hidden.
  • Delete a child's profile — in the app, under the child's profile. This permanently removes the profile and everything attached to it: measurements, goals, games, milestones, completed sessions, and uploaded photos and videos.
  • Delete your whole account — in the app, under Profile → Delete my account. This happens immediately and without us being involved. It permanently removes your login, your own training record, every child profile you own, and every photo and video you have uploaded — the files themselves, not just the records pointing at them. The email address becomes free to sign up with again from scratch, with nothing carried over. If another adult shares your household, their children stay with them and only your access is removed.
  • Revoke someone else's access — in the app, wherever you invited them.

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. Backups are rotated on a rolling basis and deleted data ages out of them.

Security

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Row-level security is enabled on every table in our database.
  • Photos and videos are private by default and served only via expiring signed links.
  • Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider; we never store or see them.
  • Administrative access to the database is restricted to the operator.

No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever affects your family's data we will tell you promptly and directly.

Your choices

  • Turn notifications off at any time in your browser or device settings.
  • Leave height, weight, and photos blank — the plan works without them.
  • Ask us what we hold, and we will tell you.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights (for example, to access, correct, port, or delete your information). Write to us and we will honour them.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change — particularly anything that would broaden how we use information about your child — we will update the date at the top and notify you in the app before it takes effect.
