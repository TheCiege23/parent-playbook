Team Connect

The stats you need. Nothing else.

A family invites you in — you get age, sport, and their current measurements, plus a way to send them workout notes, nutrition guidance, or a quick announcement. No games, journey, or photos, because that's not what a coach's relationship with the app needs to be.

Connect with a family

Ask the parent to open their athlete's Profile and send you a Team Connect invitation. Open their invitation link, enter the code they share with you, and sign in or create your own account. Each family chooses what to share and can revoke access anytime.

What you get

One screen, built around what actually changes your session plan.

📏
Current statsHeight, weight, sport, position — and every scan or timed measurement the family has recorded, kept current.
⏱
Jump, sprint, reaction, footworkThe same combine-style measurements the family sees, so you're both looking at the same numbers.
💬
Send updatesWorkout notes, nutrition guidance, or an announcement — one thread, visible to every guardian on the account.
🔒
Revocable anytimeThe family stays in control. Access can be turned off from their side at any time, no notice needed.

What you don't get

Just as important. This isn't a lighter version of the parent app — it's a different, narrower thing on purpose.

✓
Invited by the family, always There's no directory, no discovery, and no way to request access to an athlete you weren't invited to. The relationship exists in real life first — this just gives it a channel.
✕
No games, journey, or photos Those stay family-only. A coach's access is scoped at the database level, not just hidden in the interface.
✕
No messaging the athlete directly Every update goes to the family. There is no path in this app from a coach to a child.
