# Parent Playbook product and FITMATCH parity audit

**Reviewed:** 2026-09-13  
**Current weighted FITMATCH parity:** **40.75 / 100 (41% rounded)**  
**Method:** `tools/parity-progress.json`, rubric version 2. Product usability work is tracked separately and does not manufacture scientific parity credit.

## Executive finding

Parent Playbook has a working parent product, an on-device four-photo measurement workflow, a temporary clothed-surface preview, 25 provisional length outputs, growth and maturity context, Body Insights, history, reports, organization views, a portable browser SDK and an authenticated results API contract. It does not yet mirror FITMATCH's full public product claims because the remaining gaps are concentrated in scientific validation, body composition, true anatomical circumference coverage, intended-device evidence and buyer acceptance.

The parent product is stronger than the mirror target in weekly coaching, family access, games, milestones, private photos and videos, trainer discovery, camps and organization workflows. The largest Parent Playbook usability problem was that these features read as separate destinations. This pass adds a shared Plan → Growth → Journey → Settings path and puts current Journey information into Growth and current training/growth information into Journey.

## Youth scan status

The scan workflow accepts athletes under 18 when all of the following are true:

1. The athlete has a birth date.
2. A recent measured height in inches exists so photo proportions can be scaled.
3. The signed-in guardian has owner or co-parent edit authority.
4. The adult records that they are the parent or legal guardian.
5. The adult records that the child understands and agrees to the four photographs.
6. The adult accepts the measurement, image handling and 12-month retention terms.
7. Consent is still active immediately before image review, inference and save.

The child workflow is operationally eligible. Its numerical accuracy for children is not established. The current provider produces provisional estimates from clothed photos and has no independent pediatric reference study. The app must not call those values pediatric body-composition, medical, diagnostic, injury-risk or selection measurements.

## Parent Playbook priorities after this pass

1. **Run the intended-device matrix.** One current iPhone and one current Android should complete onboarding, schedule edit, youth consent, four-photo capture, save, Growth review, Journey log and consent withdrawal.
2. **Add weekly exceptions parents control.** Let a parent move, replace or skip one generated home activity while preserving rest floors and the total load budget.
3. **Make scan sessions first-class records.** The UI now groups them from provenance; a dedicated assessment table would support notes, deletion, comparison and organization transfer without reconstructing sessions from measurement rows.
4. **Improve Journey meaning.** Add sport identity to milestones and connect a saved assessment to an optional neutral milestone such as “Recorded September body measurements,” with no score or achievement language.
5. **Finish organization handoff.** Exercise the API and WebView bridge on a deployed buyer-like origin, document credential rotation and demonstrate tenant-scoped delete/export.
6. **Commission the measurement study.** Freeze output definitions first, then recruit the intended age groups, trained reference measurers and multiple devices. This is what moves the largest mirror categories.
7. **Choose the body-composition path.** A provider license is fastest. An owned system needs authorized training data, a locked model, independent DXA validation, population coverage and clear deployment rights.
