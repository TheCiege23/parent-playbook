Run photo measurements in your own application

This page uses the standalone browser SDK and the same length-estimation engine as Parent Playbook. No account or database is connected. Photos are processed on this device; the pose model is downloaded from Google and jsDelivr.

SDK 0.13.0 · Provisional: up to 25 defined length and landmark-distance estimates, plus an optional experimental 3D surface from four reviewed silhouettes. Shape and measurement accuracy are unvalidated. No anatomical girths or body composition are computed.

HEIC/iPhone originals convert to PNG on this device after you confirm permission. Check each preview before reviewing. Keep clear space below both feet and above the head; do not crop shoes or toes.

Try an adult reference scan

Brace the phone vertically at hip height about eight feet away. Mark the phone position and one standing center on the floor, keep the same zoom, and have the subject rotate in place for separate front, right, back and left photos. Keep the head and both feet visible, with fitted sports clothing and even lighting. Direction checks are advisory. JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 25 MB and 16 megapixels each.

The 3D option adds manual silhouette review and processing time. This demo keeps geometry in memory only and clears it when the result changes, you select Clear, or you leave. The JSON download contains only the length assessment.
