How consistent are two scans?

Compare geometric sections from two separately captured experimental surfaces. Matching results do not prove accuracy: both surfaces can share the same error.

Use two independent captures

In the reconstruction lab, capture the same adult twice in one session using the same pose, clothing, measured height and resolution. Take four new photos for the second scan. Download the OBJ and method JSON for each scan. Choose horizontal levels measured from the floor before inspecting the differences.
