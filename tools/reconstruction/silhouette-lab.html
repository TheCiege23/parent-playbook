Experimental reconstruction — accuracy unverified

This captures the visible outer silhouette, including clothing and hair. It assumes upright, matching poses and orthographic views. It cannot recover hidden anatomy, body composition or validated circumferences. It is not FITMATCH-equivalent technology.

Photos, silhouettes and reopened geometry stay in browser memory. Model code and weights download from jsDelivr and Google. There is no upload or account save. Explicit downloads retain sensitive geometry on your device; Clear releases this page’s photo/mask/model references. Keep minors in the existing consent-controlled app workflow.

Prepare your reference photos and measurements
  1. Wear fitted athletic clothing, stand barefoot and tie long hair up. Use a plain background with even lighting. Have someone else take the photos or use a fixed phone support.
  2. Keep the phone level, at approximately mid-body height, using the normal 1× camera. Keep the same camera position and distance for all four shots; turn your whole body in place. Leave space above your head and below your feet.
  3. Stand upright with arms slightly away from the torso and feet in a consistent position. Front means facing the camera; right means your right side faces the camera; back means facing away; left means your left side faces the camera. View direction is checked by you.
  4. Record your standing height. To test a perimeter later, choose a clearly identifiable horizontal level around your torso. Record a tape measurement around that level and its vertical height from the floor, both in cm. Use the same clothing and pose, and record the location before viewing the model result.
  5. Choose each photo below. JPEG, PNG and WebP work; HEIC originals convert to PNG on this device. The first photo may take longer while the model downloads. Clear releases this session, so keep your originals if you want to repeat the test.

Height sets model scale and cannot independently prove accuracy. The optional tape comparison can reveal an error at one selected level. One person or one comparison cannot validate the product.
