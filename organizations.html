An organization account is being designed for the person running a league, a club, or a school program — not one athlete at a time, but the whole roster, and the coaches connected to it.

In design — not live yet

What's planned

Confirmed direction, not a finished feature — this page will update as it's actually built.

👥
Manage the whole roster See the athletes and families connected to your organization in one place, instead of one Team Connect invite at a time.
🎓
Oversee your coaches Know which coaches are connected to which athletes under your organization — an admin layer above individual Team Connect access, not a replacement for it.
🏕️
List your own camps & programs Manage your organization's listings in the camps directory directly, rather than a single parent submitting one on your behalf.

Same rule as everywhere else in Parent Playbook: an organization sees what it needs to run training and communication, never more. Whatever an organization can see about an athlete will be at most what an individual coach can see today — stats and measurements, never games, journey, or photos — that scope won't loosen as this gets built.

Tell us what you'd actually need

This is being designed now. If you run a league, club, or school and want to help shape it — how many athletes, how many coaches, what a roster view should actually show — we want to hear it before it's built, not after.
