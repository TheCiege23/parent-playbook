Parent Playbook · September 2026

Body intelligence that stays attached to the athlete's journey.

A four-view photo scan inside a product parents already use for training, rest and schedule — and an organization side that can hold the results properly.

  • For parents: 25 provisional length and proportion outputs, growth and maturity context, printable report, CSV and JSON export.
  • For organizations: rostered athletes, consent, organization-scoped records with full audit and bounded retention.
  • For a buyer: a portable SDK, its migrations and its verification tooling, hostable on your own origin.
  • Release candidate: build pp-v145; production verification follows deployment.
Notes. Lead with the journey, not the measurement. Do not open on parity percentages. If asked what is live right now: capture, outputs, insights, organization records and the SDK are live; composition is gated off and produces nothing.

Slide 3

The live sequence

  1. Consent check, then four guided views — front, right, back, left.
  2. Automatic per-photo review: person detection, landmark visibility, frame-edge and coverage checks, exact-duplicate rejection.
  3. On-device pose measurement returning 25 of 25 provisional outputs, with recorded wall-clock processing time.
  4. Body Insights: outputs, bilateral pairs and differences, growth context, printable report, CSV and JSON export.
  5. Organization view: the same record saved, reopened, exported and deleted — every mutation audited, every record given a retention date.
Notes. Rehearse on the presentation device and browser beforehand, and rehearse a rejected scan too, so a failure on the day looks like a designed path rather than a crash. Processing time is client-recorded and includes model load on a cold run — never present it as a benchmark against anyone.

Slide 4

Data operation — the part that is actually finished

  • Tenant isolation lives in the database, not only the app. Row-level security, organization-admin policies, a scalar-only payload allowlist, and privileges that deny TRUNCATE to every tenant role.
  • 204 executable database tests against a disposable PostgreSQL cluster built by replaying the real 81 migrations — not assertions about SQL source text.
  • Deletion is rehearsed, not asserted: the row goes, the deletion is audited, the audit row keeps no copy of the values, the scheduled sweep removes expired records, and another organization cannot delete yours.
  • Restore is rehearsed: the schema dumps and restores with row-level security and privileges intact — and the rehearsal damages the restored copy on purpose to prove it would notice if they were not.
  • The buyer archive stands alone: 156 files, checksummed, verified by one command; its own bundled migrations rebuild the schema and pass all 204 tests on a clean database.
Notes. The strongest slide — the one claim backed by executed tests rather than judgment. Three defects worth admitting if pressed, because finding them is the point: the SQL payload allowlist accepted a record with a missing outputs key (a NULL comparison), a provider could register declaring zero outputs, and 51 production tables granted TRUNCATE to tenant roles — which bypasses row-level security entirely. All three were found by executing the tests, and all three are fixed and covered.

Slide 5

Real-person surface evidence — and what it is not

  • One authorized adult reference set completed the full SDK flow: 25 of 25 provisional outputs and a rotatable experimental surface.
  • Strict reconstruction found 4,770 connected body voxels plus a detached 14-voxel artifact. The opt-in bounded removal took out that 0.29% fragment without filling or joining geometry, and recorded exact counts.
  • Three pose-guided plane sections measured, with view spread recorded. At the hip plane, three contours were found and only the unique contour containing the reconstruction axis was measured.
What this is not. These are clothed-surface perimeters, not validated anatomical girths. Anatomical accuracy, repeatability, intended-phone behavior and buyer-host deployment are unverified. No composition value has ever been produced — the provider gate reports Model not configured and generates nothing.
Notes. Say the limits out loud rather than leaving them in small type; a buyer who finds a limit themselves discounts everything else on the slide. Composition requires an owned, licensed or buyer-supplied model with DEXA training provenance and independent validation evidence. We built the gate that would accept one and the evaluator that would score it. We have not claimed, and will not claim, DEXA equivalence or clinical validity.

Slide 6

Where this goes, and what it needs

Now: capture, 25 outputs, growth and maturity, organization records, portable SDK — deployed.

  • A licensed composition model plus its independent DEXA evidence — unlocks FM05, the single heaviest gap at 15 points.
  • An agreement study against a reference protocol — unlocks percentiles and circumference claims.
  • Buyer-host acceptance and a physical-device rehearsal.

Each of these is blocked on an input, not on engineering. Around the core: movement video, trainer and camp discovery, organization analytics.

Notes. Close on what the buyer supplies rather than what we still owe. Population reference comparisons are deliberately switched off: the app shows a plain explanation instead of a percentile, because pose-derived estimates have not been shown to measure the same quantity as tape-and-caliper anthropometry. Hand over REFERENCE-COHORTS.md — it demonstrates the standard being applied rather than merely described.

Appendix

Limits to disclose, and how to re-derive every number

  • No validated anatomical circumference, no body-composition output, no claim of FIT:MATCH accuracy equivalence, no DEXA equivalence, no clinical validity.
  • PHV and maturity offset are manual anthropometric estimates with real population and individual limits.
  • Measurement dates are not scan-session identifiers; historical records lack complete provenance.
  • The trainer marketplace exists in code but is disabled pending vendor and legal review.
  • Assessment captures are discarded; no retained training dataset is implied.
