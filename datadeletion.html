Data Deletion

How to permanently delete a single measurement, a child's profile, or your entire Parent Playbook account and everything in it. Deletion happens immediately and does not require contacting us.

Last updated 14 September 2026

The short version. You control deletion yourself, from inside the app, at any time. There is no waiting period and no approval step. Deleting a child's profile or your account removes the underlying records and files — not just a flag that hides them.

Delete a single measurement

This permanently removes one reading, such as a height, a sprint time or a jump, from Growth, Body Insights and everyone you have shared stats with.

  1. Log in and open the athlete's Growth tab.
  2. Tap the measurement, then Delete on the reading you want gone, and confirm.

Where a set of readings was saved together as one body scan, Growth sends you to the Scan tab instead, where the whole scan is deleted at once, so a scan is never left partly deleted.

Delete a child's profile

This permanently removes that child's profile and everything attached to it: measurements (including any body-scan or jump-measurement results), goals, games, milestones, completed sessions, and any photos or videos you uploaded for them.

  1. Log in and open the child's profile.
  2. Open Profile for that athlete.
  3. Choose Delete profile and confirm.

If another guardian shares this athlete with you, deleting the profile removes it for everyone with access to it, not just you. If you only want to remove your own access and leave the profile with the other guardian, use Revoke access below instead.

Delete your entire account

This immediately and permanently removes:

  • Your login and password.
  • Your own training record, if you keep one: goals, schedule, workouts, measurements, food preferences, recipes and meal plans.
  • Every child profile you own — measurements, goals, games, milestones, and completed sessions.
  • Every photo and video you uploaded — the actual files, not just the database records pointing at them.
  1. Log in and go to Profile → Account.
  2. Choose Delete my account and confirm.

This happens without us being involved on our end — there is no support ticket to file and no delay. The email address becomes free to sign up with again from scratch, with nothing carried over. If you share a household with another guardian, their children stay with them; only your own login and the profiles you personally own are removed.

Revoke a co-parent's or grandparent's access

To remove someone you invited without deleting the athlete itself, open that athlete's profile, find them under shared access, and revoke. This takes effect immediately — it does not delete anything they may have added themselves while they had access; delete those items individually, or delete the profile if you want everything gone.

Can't log in?

If you've lost access to your account and can't use the in-app deletion tools above, email support@playbookbp.com from the address on the account (or with whatever proof of identity you can provide) and ask us to delete it. We will verify you're the account holder and delete the account and its data by hand — this path exists only as a fallback for when the self-serve option isn't reachable, and typically takes a few business days rather than being immediate.

What deletion does not touch

  • Backups. We keep rolling backups for disaster recovery. Deleted data ages out of these on our normal rotation rather than being scrubbed from every backup the instant you delete it — see our Privacy Policy for details.
  • Body scan and jump-measurement photos/video. These were never uploaded or stored in the first place — they're processed on your own phone and discarded within seconds. There is nothing to delete here beyond the measurement numbers themselves, which are covered above.
  • Records we're legally required to keep, if any — we don't currently hold any such records for family accounts.
